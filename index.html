What A2 Digi Growth does

Pick one thing or the whole set. Every project starts with a conversation about what your business actually needs.

Static websites
Fast, simple sites for shops, clinics, schools and services. Your address, timings, products and a WhatsApp button, loading in under a second on any phone.
Shopify e-commerce stores
Full online stores with payments, shipping, collections and inventory. Set up, themed to your brand, and handed over with training so you can run it yourself.
Poster and creative design
Festival offers, product launches, menus and social media posts, designed to match your brand and sized for WhatsApp, Instagram and print.
Digital marketing
Instagram, Facebook and Google ads with clear budgets and monthly reports. You see what was spent and what came back.
SEO
Get found when people nearby search for what you sell. Google Business Profile, on-page fixes, local keywords and content that ranks.
Solar scheme applications
Help with rooftop solar subsidy and net-metering applications: documents, portal registration and follow-up until approval.

How a project goes

  1. We talk

    A call or WhatsApp chat about your business, customers and budget. You get a fixed quote and timeline in writing, usually within two days.

  2. I build, you review

    You see the design early and the live preview often. Content, photos and changes go back and forth until it feels right.

  3. Launch and handover

    Domain, hosting and Google setup done for you, with a walkthrough so you can update prices and posts yourself. I stay reachable after launch.
